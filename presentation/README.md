# MARKOUT UHI10 Presentation

`MARKOUT-UHI10.pptx` is the nine-slide Reactive-first judge deck. Its narrative moves from the fee-classification gap
through the outcome-priced mechanism, deterministic evidence, Reactive Network event-to-action architecture,
engineering proof, and the demonstrated allocation outcomes.

## Architecture position

- Reactive Network is the primary cross-chain event-to-action layer.
- The deployed Legacy pulse subscribes to the exact Pyth-backed publisher and market event.
- ReactVM validates and encodes the authenticated Unichain callback.
- The Unichain hook alone owns custody, evidence validation, fee calculation, and terminal accounting.
- Permissionless expiry returns the full provisional amount if delivery is unavailable.
- Earlier Circle lifecycles remain historical mechanism evidence and are not presented as the current primary path.

## Evidence policy

- Every quantitative value comes from the committed Phase 6 trade tape and generated reports.
- The deck calls the loss metric a post-trade adverse-selection proxy, not exact LVR or individual LP PnL.
- The volatility-policy regression is visible rather than hidden.
- The final slide records the demonstrated full-rebate and full-retention branches.
- The deck credits Reactive with a public 11-second authenticated callback and does not relabel that terminal-trade
  callback as a pending-first economic settlement.
- Speaker notes contain a `[Sources]` block on every slide.
